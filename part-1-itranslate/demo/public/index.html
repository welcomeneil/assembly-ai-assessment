Streaming is billed on how long the connection stays open, not on how much audio goes through it. The amber stretch is the device still connected after the conversation ended, charged at the same rate as speech.

On a handheld that spends most of its life in a pocket this is the largest cost lever there is — larger than the choice of model. Worth deciding the hang-up policy before the first bill, not after.
